How to read this. Everything drawn here is reduced from trace.jsonl: the page replays a record, it is not a window on a running simulator, and what was not traced cannot appear. The line under the title is the trace header; read it first, since every panel is conditional on it.

charts asks what is binding: the whole network first, then one followed transaction and one object below the scope bar. network shows where that transaction is known: nodes coloured by the evidence they hold, categories rather than a scale, green edges for how it spread, red arrows for deliveries that taught their receiver nothing. transaction puts the same transaction on its own clock, one row per node in the order they learned of it.

auto follow picks the transaction and moves on when its story ends or after the set number of slots; choosing a transaction pins it, and ‹ › step through the list. Every panel's ? says what it draws and how; its table gives the values behind the pixels.
